# Home hero rotation: six scenes

One image prompt and one video prompt per scene. Each prompt is complete on its
own: paste it as is.

**Images:** any image generator. Ask for 16:9.

**Video:** Wan 2.2 image-to-video, free on Hugging Face (free account: 5 GPU
minutes a day, Apache 2.0, no watermark):
https://huggingface.co/spaces/zerogpu-aoti/wan2-2-fp8da-aoti-faster
Upload the image, paste the video prompt, set Duration to 5.0 and, under
Advanced Settings, Inference Steps to 8. Leave the negative prompt as it is.
If a take is wrong, run it again with a new seed.

---

## 1. Fulfillment warehouse

**Image prompt**

> A wide 16:9 editorial illustration in flat mid-century modernist style, like a 1960s corporate annual report cover or a vintage screen print, showing the interior of a tall, sunlit fulfillment warehouse with a long skylight running down the ceiling. The left third of the image is filled by tall steel racking stacked with crates, with a robotic picking arm mounted on the racking, lifting a crate off a shelf. The right third is an industrial palletizing robot arm stacking boxes onto a pallet. Along the bottom fifth of the image, two low autonomous mobile robots carry shelving pods across a polished floor, heading in opposite directions, each topped with a small orange beacon light. The entire center of the image, about half its width and the middle half of its height, is the far back wall of the warehouse: one plain, flat, empty expanse of warm cream with nothing on it, no lamps, no signs, no lines and no texture detail, because a headline will be set there. Every robot stands apart from the others with clear space around it and is caught mid-task. Use strictly four colors: warm cream paper (#FFFEF2) for the walls, skylight and most of the image, muted sage green (#7D9C90) for the floor and mid-tones, deep forest green (#1C4439) for the robots, racking, steel and shadows, and burnt terracotta orange (#C25A38) only for the small beacon lights. The image is bright and high key, never dark. Flat shapes with no outlines, long hard-edged geometric shadows cast by the skylight, heavy paper grain and a faint risograph misregistration texture. No people, no text, no lettering, no numbers, no logos, no screens, no 3D render look.

**Video prompt**

> A locked-off static camera on a tripod; the frame never moves, pans or zooms. Only the robots move, slowly and smoothly. The picking arm on the left lifts its crate a little higher and swings it a short distance toward the camera. The palletizing arm on the right lowers a box onto the stack and rises again. The two mobile robots on the floor glide a short distance in opposite directions. Their small orange beacon lights blink. The racking, crates, walls, skylight, floor and shadows stay perfectly still, the lighting does not change, and no new objects or people appear. The motion is calm and ends close to where it began, so the clip can loop.
