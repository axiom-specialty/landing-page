# Cover image prompts

Every prompt below is self-contained: copy one, paste it, generate. The house
style is repeated inside each prompt on purpose, so you never have to paste two
things.

**Where the files go:** `public/covers/` in this repo.
**What to name them:** the "Save as" line under each prompt. The name is the
product slug and it must match exactly, or the card falls back to the
placeholder.

**After generating:** downloads arrive as `.png` or `.webp` with a random name.
Rename to the exact `.jpg` name given. Renaming a PNG to `.jpg` is fine here,
browsers render by content, not extension. Existing art in that folder is
1400x875.

## 1. Homepage hero

Save as: `home-hero.jpg`

> A wide landscape editorial illustration in flat mid-century modernist style, like a 1960s corporate annual report cover or a vintage screen print. One continuous panoramic scene reading left to right across three zones that share a single horizon: at left, a robotic arm and a small autonomous machine on a factory floor; at center, a line of electrical transmission pylons receding toward the horizon; at right, a large softly glowing geometric plane of fine parallel light lines, suggesting a computational field. The three zones connect through the shared ground plane and a single unbroken horizon line, so it reads as one landscape rather than three panels. Strictly limited palette: warm cream paper (#FFFEF2) for the sky and negative space, deep forest green (#1C4439) for the ground and silhouettes, muted sage green (#7D9C90) for the mid-tones, and exactly one burnt terracotta orange (#C25A38) used sparingly, as a low sun on the horizon. Flat vector shapes with no outlines, hard-edged geometric shadows raking long across the ground from a low light source, subtle paper grain and a faint risograph print texture over everything. Calm, institutional, confident, restrained. Generous negative space in the upper third of the frame. No text, no lettering, no logos, no signage, no people's faces, no lens flare, no 3D render look, no neon, no cyberpunk. 16:10 wide landscape.

## If a result drifts off-style

The two things that go wrong most often, and what to append:

- Too 3D or too glossy → append: "Absolutely flat, two-dimensional, no gradients except a single soft glow, no specular highlights, no depth of field."
- Too many colors → append: "Use only four colors in the entire image: #FFFEF2, #1C4439, #7D9C90, and #C25A38. No other hues."
