    Underwriting

    01 / 04

    Broker submission

    Work with your broker to fill in the application: the robots you run, where they work, who shares the space with them, and how they are maintained. If your robot vendor offers our cover, it can be included when you buy or lease the robots instead.

    Quote and bind

    We review your application and come back with a quote through your broker. Accept it, and your fleet is covered!

    Some applications may be declined.

    Risk mitigation

    Your policy comes with AuxControl, a suite of risk mitigation and governance tools that gives you a clear picture of your fleet's exposure. Fleets of 10 or more robots connect a read-only data feed within 30 days, so patterns like repeated emergency stops or a troublesome software release come to light early and are fixed before they cost you anything.

    Renewal

    Ahead of renewal, your broker receives a renewal application that already reflects what we know about your fleet, so there is less to fill in. The work you put in during the year counts: safer operations and stronger controls are taken into account in your renewal terms. New robots, sites and vendors are added at the same time, so your cover keeps pace with your fleet.

    Regulatory frameworks

    ANSI/A3 R15.08

    The US safety standard for industrial mobile robots, from the robot itself to how it is integrated and used on site.

    ISO 10218

    Safety requirements for industrial robots and robot systems, revised in 2025 to take in collaborative operation.

    ISO 3691-4

    Safety for driverless industrial trucks and their systems, including autonomous forklifts and mobile robots.

    UL 3100

    The safety standard for automated mobile platforms, covering their electrical systems, batteries and charging.

    ISO/TS 15066

    Guidance for collaborative robots working alongside people, including limits on contact force and pressure.

    FM Data Sheet 8-34

    Loss prevention guidance for lithium-ion batteries, which we apply to charging areas: spacing, detection and suppression.
